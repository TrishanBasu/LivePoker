Texas Hold'em Hand Rankings & Rules

  1. Royal Flush: A-K-Q-J-10 suited.
  2. Straight Flush: 5 consecutive suited cards.
  3. Four of a Kind: 4 cards of the same rank.
  4. Full House: 3 of a kind + a pair.
  5. Flush: 5 cards of the same suit.
  6. Straight: 5 consecutive cards of mixed suits.
  7. Three of a Kind: 3 cards of the same rank.
  8. Two Pair: 2 distinct pairs + kicker.
  9. One Pair: 1 pair + 3 kickers.
