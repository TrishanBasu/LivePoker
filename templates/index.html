Texas Hold'em Rulebook & Hand Rankings

1. Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)

  1. Royal Flush: A-K-Q-J-10 all of the exact same suit. Unbeatable.
  2. Straight Flush: Five consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 9♠ 8♠ 7♠ 6♠ 5♠).
  3. Four of a Kind: All four cards of one rank (e.g., Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ Q♣ 4♠).
  4. Full House: Three cards of one rank plus a pair of another (e.g., K♠ K♦ K♣ 7♠ 7♥).
  5. Flush: Any five cards of the same suit, not in sequence. Highest card breaks ties.
  6. Straight: Five consecutive cards of mixed suits. Ace can play high (10-J-Q-K-A) or low (A-2-3-4-5).
  7. Three of a Kind: Three cards of the same rank plus two unrelated kickers.
  8. Two Pair: Two different pairs plus one kicker card.
  9. One Pair: Two cards of matching rank plus three highest kickers.
  10. High Card: When no pair or better is made, the highest card in your best 5-card hand plays.

2. Kickers & Tie-Breaking

Every showdown compares the best 5-card hand out of your 2 private cards + 5 community cards. If two players hold the same pair or trips, the highest remaining side cards (kickers) decide the winner. If all 5 cards are identical in rank, the pot is split equally (Split Pot).

3. Blinds, Betting Rounds & Actions

  • Blinds: Forced bets posted before cards are dealt by the two players left of the Dealer Button (Small Blind & Big Blind).
  • Rounds: Pre-Flop (2 hole cards) → Flop (3 board cards) → Turn (4th card) → River (5th card) → Showdown.
  • Check: Pass action with 0 bet when no one has bet in the current round.
  • Call: Match the current highest bet to stay in the hand.
  • Bet / Raise: Put chips in to open betting or increase an existing bet.
  • Fold: Discard your hand and forfeit any chips already contributed to the pot.
  • All-In & Side Pots: If you bet all your remaining chips, you are eligible to win up to your contribution from every player. Extra chips bet by deeper stacks form a separate Side Pot contested only by those remaining players.
